Privacy

This game stores everything on your device. We don't collect any information.

For grown-ups

  • Game progress is saved only in this browser on this device.
  • There are no accounts, no chat, no ads, no analytics, and no trackers.
  • The game never sends your child's name, pets, or anything else over the internet.
  • Fonts and art are bundled with the game, so it makes no requests to other websites.
  • Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes the saves on this device.
